# 📌 Project Overview

The **AURA Coffee Executive Sales Dashboard** is an interactive Business Intelligence solution built using **Power BI** and **MySQL**. The dashboard enables business stakeholders to monitor sales performance, identify revenue trends, evaluate product and store performance, and understand customer purchasing patterns.

The objective of this project is to transform raw transactional sales data into meaningful business insights that support strategic decision-making.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

Coffee shop managers often rely on multiple reports to monitor business performance, making it difficult to identify trends and make timely decisions.

This dashboard addresses that challenge by providing a centralized executive view of:

- Sales Performance
- Order Volume
- Product Performance
- Store Performance
- Sales Trends
- Customer Purchase Behaviour

allowing stakeholders to make faster, data-driven decisions.

---

# 🎯 Project Objectives

The dashboard was designed to answer the following business questions:

- How much revenue was generated?
- How many orders were placed?
- How many products were sold?
- Which products generate the highest sales?
- Which product categories perform best?
- Which store location generates the most revenue?
- How do weekday and weekend sales compare?
- During which hours are customers most active?
- How has sales performance changed throughout the month?

# 📈 Executive KPIs

The dashboard monitors three primary executive KPIs:

| KPI | Description |
|------|-------------|
| 💰 Total Sales | Total revenue generated during the selected period |
| 🛒 Total Orders | Total number of customer orders |
| ☕ Total Quantity Sold | Total products sold |

Each KPI also includes:

- Month-over-Month Growth
- Previous Month Comparison
- Daily Sales Trend (Sparkline)

# 💡 Business Insights

Key insights derived from the dashboard include:

- Revenue increased significantly compared to the previous month.
- Coffee products contributed the highest sales among all categories.
- Hell's Kitchen emerged as the highest-performing store location.
- Weekday sales exceeded weekend sales.
- Customer activity peaked during specific daytime hours.
- Sales declined towards the end of the month, indicating a potential opportunity for promotional campaigns.
